─── §1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ──────────────────────────────────────────

PURPOSE: A scalar, modular, multiuse ONE-PIECE dwelling that is a
mobile caravan, a fixed unmobile home, and — when stacked and
interlocked — a condo. It meets ISO 668 cargo-container dimensions,
carries zero patent infringement, cleans and sterilizes itself at the
press of one button, seals airtight under resident-only control with
fail-open ventilation, floats in flood, and is manufactured in tiers
from affordable mass-producible recycled plastic to advanced
expand/contract models.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
  External envelope ......... ISO 668 20ft: 6058 × 2438 × 2591 mm
  Internal clear ............. 5898 × 2352 × 2393 mm (nominal)
  Corner castings ............ ISO 1161 (178 × 162 × 118 mm) — stacking
  Shell ...................... Monocoque rotomolded, 60–80 mm wall
  Airtightness ............... < 0.5 ACH @ 50 Pa (quantified, not absolute)
  Sealing control ............ RESIDENT-ONLY (interior deadbolt + gasket)
  Ventilation ................ Fail-open spring louvres + pressure-relief
  Cleaning ................... One-button mist (interior) + hose (exterior)
  Buoyancy ................... Positive (sealed hull, top egress hatch)
  Energy ..................... Solar PV + AFPM + battery + non-electrical
  Manufacture ................ Tier 1 rotomold → Tier 3 expand/contract

EFE FILTER: 7/7 PASS (Sustainable, Renewable, Accessible, Open,
Local-First, Circular, Durable) — evidenced in §6 and §7.

OPTIBEST: 7 dimensions × score 5/5, plateau-verified (§9).

2.2 STRUCTURAL
  Shell ......... Monocoque rotomolded shell, single-piece (no seams)
  Wall ......... 60 mm (Tier 1) / 80 mm (Tier 3) foam-core sandwich
  Floor ......... Integrated, sloped 1:100 to central drain channel
  Roof ......... Integrated, sloped 1:50 to perimeter gutter
  Corner posts .. Steel ISO corner castings bonded into shell
  Rigidity ...... Racking resistance ≥ 150 kN (ISO 1496-1 test)
  Stability ..... Self-righting when floating (low CG, ballast keel)

2.3 AIRTIGHTNESS + RESIDENT SAFETY (PRIORITY 1)
  Seal ......... EPDM compression gasket on door + all penetrations
  Sealing ...... INTERIOR-ONLY deadbolt (3-point) compresses gasket
  EXTERIOR ..... No sealing mechanism exists on the outside — a
                 person outside CANNOT seal or lock a resident in.
  Ventilation .. Fail-open spring louvres: open when unpowered or
                 when ΔP across shell exceeds ±250 Pa (pressure-relief)
  CO2/O2 ....... Passive monitoring; louvres auto-open at CO2 > 1000 ppm
  Egress ....... Top hatch (600 × 600 mm) operable from inside, always
  Redundancy ... Two independent vent paths + one PRV + one hatch

2.4 CLEANING / STERILIZATION (ONE-BUTTON)
  Interior ..... Ceiling mist-nozzle array (6 nozzles) + wall wash ring
  Exterior ..... Hose fitting (standard 3/4" BSP) + perimeter spray ring
  Agent ........ User-supplied antibacterial canister, standard lid
  Drain ........ Central floor drain → greywater tank (or external)
  Surfaces ..... All non-porous, IPX5-rated, sloped to drain
  Cycle ........ Mist (5 min) → dwell (10 min) → rinse (3 min) → dry

2.5 FLOOD-FLOAT
  Hull ......... Sealed monocoque, all penetrations above waterline
  Buoyancy ..... Displacement > 1.5 × tare mass (positive margin)
  Trim ......... Ballast keel + self-righting geometry
  Egress ....... Top hatch doubles as emergency exit when afloat

2.6 UTILITIES
  Kitchen ...... Compact induction (electric) + alcohol stove (fallback)
  Bedding ...... Fold-down berth, 2-person
  Ablutions .... Wet-room with drain, hand-pump + gravity shower
  Storage ...... Integrated wall cavities + under-floor bins
  Water ........ 200 L potable tank + filtration + greywater tank
  Electricity .. 12 V DC bus + 230 V AC inverter

2.7 ENERGY
  Solar ........ 1.2 kWp roof PV (interchangeable)
  AFPM ......... Axial-flux permanent-magnet generator, self-starting
  Battery ...... LiFePO4 5 kWh (interchangeable)
  Fallback ..... Non-electrical: gravity water, passive vent, manual pump

4.3 DOOR + SEAL (resident-only)
   EXTERIOR face: flush, no handle, no lock — nothing to grip/seal
   INTERIOR face: 3-point deadbolt → compresses EPDM gasket
   FAIL-OPEN: spring louvres default OPEN; seal only when resident
              actively engages deadbolt from inside.

─── §5. MANUFACTURING SPECIFICATION ────────────────────────────────

5.1 TIER 1 — ENTRY (affordable, mass-producible)
  Process ..... Rotational molding, single-piece shell
  Material .... Recycled HDPE (EFE Tier 1)
  Tooling ..... Single rotomold tool (steel), amortized over 10k units
  Cycle ....... ~45 min/shell
  QC .......... Wall-thickness ultrasonic scan, leak test @ 250 Pa
  Skill ....... Low (rotomolding is semi-skilled)

5.2 TIER 2 — STANDARD
  Adds ........ Solar PV + battery + AFPM + insulation + mist system
  Process ..... Shell (rotomold) + fit-out (modular cassettes)

5.3 TIER 3 — ADVANCED (expand/contract)
  Adds ........ Telescoping side panels (transport 2438 mm → deploy
                +600 mm each side) while maintaining seal + rigidity
  Process ..... Shell + sliding cassette modules + re-sealable joints

5.4 SCALE PLAN
  Prototype (1–10) → Batch (10–100) → Production (100–1k) →
  Mass (1k+) → Global distributed (rotomold nodes near demand)
